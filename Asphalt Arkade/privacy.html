2. Besucherstatistik mit GoatCounter

Diese Seite nutzt GoatCounter (Instanz: kroxenstudios.goatcounter.com), um anonym zu zählen, wie oft das Spiel aufgerufen wird. Das Skript wird von gc.zgo.at geladen.

Dabei können folgende Angaben verarbeitet werden:

Privacy Policy

In short: Asphalt Arkade counts visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly statistics service. No cookies are set, no user profiles are created and no data is used for advertising. Your game progress stays in your browser.

1. Controller

KROX Electronics / KroxenStudios
Contact: kroxenstudios@proton.me

2. Visitor statistics with GoatCounter

This site uses GoatCounter (instance: kroxenstudios.goatcounter.com) to anonymously count how often the game is opened. The script is loaded from gc.zgo.at.

The following information may be processed:

  • page visited and referrer (where you come from)
  • browser, operating system and screen size
  • approximate location (country), derived from the IP address

Your IP address is not stored. GoatCounter sets no cookies and does not use local storage to recognise you. There is no tracking across multiple pages or devices. The data is not passed on to third parties for advertising purposes.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in anonymous reach measurement (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

3. Game

The game runs entirely in your browser. Inputs, balance and game progress are not transmitted to a server.

4. Hosting

When the site is accessed, the hosting provider processes technically necessary access data (e.g. IP address, time) in server logs in order to deliver the site.

5. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure and objection. Simply write to the address above. You can also block the statistics, e.g. with a content blocker or by enabling "Do Not Track".
