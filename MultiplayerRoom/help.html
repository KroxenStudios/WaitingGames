Bastelkarre

Build a vehicle from parts on your parking lot and drive around with the other players. Back to the game

Building

Place a partPick a part on the left, then left-click a surface or the floor of the parking lot. The preview is green = connected, yellow = loose, red = not possible.
Remove a partRight-click (or the “Remove” tool)
Adjust a partMiddle-click or Alt+click (or the “Adjust” tool): colour, keys, steering angle, thrust …
Rotate / tiltR rotates the next part, T tilts blocks and wedges. Attachments (wheels, exhaust, engines …) align to the surface you click.
ViewDrag to rotate, right-drag or Shift+drag to pan, mouse wheel to zoom, WASD to move.
Check connectionsV colours every rigid body differently. Everything in one colour is firmly connected.
UndoCtrl+Z
PresetsThe “Presets” button in the build panel: ready-made vehicles (kart, dragster, shopping cart, off-roader, plane, crane truck, truck with trailer, plus the bigger projects: coach with 13 seats, space rocket, propeller plane, fire truck with ladder and water tank, propeller buggy and a train with two detachable cars). They replace your current vehicle; undo brings it back.

Parts connect when they touch with connecting faces. Unconnected parts don't move with the vehicle – unless a force acts on them (a loose engine flies off on its own). Your design is saved in the browser.

Parts

SeatThis is where you sit. You can build as many seats as you like. The first seat built defines “forward” for throttle and steering. Press X to get out and back in – into any free seat nearby, even in other players' vehicles (then you ride along). Or just click (or tap) a seat. Only your own vehicle is steered, and only while you sit in one of its seats.
Block, slab, wedge, corner wedgeBody and frame.
Steerable wheelSteers with A/D; maximum steering angle adjustable. Wheels behind the centre of mass steer in the opposite direction automatically (adjustable).
Fixed wheelOnly rolls straight. Driven or free-rolling.
Caster wheelSwivels freely through 360° in the direction of travel, no drive.
ExhaustExhaust smoke while the engine runs (M). Colour adjustable.
Fog generatorFog at the press of a key (default F), even with the engine off. Colour adjustable.
Jet engineContinuous thrust while the key is held (default Shift).
Rocket engineVery strong thrust for a few seconds (default R), then it recharges.
WingFlat plate that creates lift and drag in the airflow. Lift needs an angle of attack (nose slightly up or the surface tilted); above about 17° the flow stalls. Effective area adjustable.
Control surfaceLike the wing, but its trailing edge (yellow) can be tilted. Channel Pitch (elevator, ↓ = nose up), Roll (aileron, ←→, set left and right to “opposite”) or Yaw (rudder, built upright, QE). Deflection and keys adjustable. The arrow keys no longer steer wheels – use WASD for that.
CameraA key (default V, further cameras B, N …) switches the view to that camera; press again to return. The camera looks in the direction of its lens, moves with its part and also works when you got out or the camera lies around loose. Field of view adjustable.
HeadlightShines in the direction of the lens; the key (default L) switches it on and off. Colour adjustable. The four nearest lit headlights cast a real light cone.
LadderClimb on foot: at the ladder press W/S, Space jumps off. Several stacked make a long ladder; rotate it with R/T.
Propeller engineThrust like a propeller: G more, H less throttle (the setting stays). Thrust falls with speed (top speed adjustable) and in thin air, and there is none in space. Fast-spinning blades blur into a disc.
Fuel tankFuel for jet engines, propellers and the drive motor (rockets have their own). If a vehicle has tanks, nothing runs without fuel – each part only gets fuel from tanks firmly connected to it. Without a tank, fuel use is off. K switches fuel use off and on (fuel is then unlimited). The fill level is shown next to the speed.
ParachuteA key (default P) opens it, press again to pack it. Brakes through air drag: the faster, the stronger – also when falling after jumps. Size and colour adjustable.
Servo motorWhatever hangs on the turntable on top turns against the rest (default Q/E). Mode “hold angle” with a limit, or “continuous rotation”.
Breakaway jointHolds both sides firmly together until you press its key (default 1, 2, …). Then the parts separate.

The map

Around the flat arena with the parking lots lies a large landscape of 6.4 × 6.4 km: hills, valleys, mountains, forests, roads, eight villages and radio towers. The map was generated once and is the same for all players. A ring of mountains surrounds it. The roads lead from the arena to the villages; you can take off with the plane from the runway (east of the obstacles).

Altitude and space

The air gets thinner with altitude: wings, control surfaces and parachute lose their effect, rockets and engines do not. Between roughly 3000 and 7000 m gravity fades away; above that you are weightless in black space with stars and a small Earth. Your altitude is shown next to your speed. If you sink again, gravity pulls you back from about 3000 m.

Online

Up to 12 players, everyone gets a parking lot. Each browser simulates the physics of its own vehicle and sends the position of its parts to the server; the others see them and can drive into them. Name and colour come from the character page. Nothing is stored on the server.
