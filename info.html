Waiting Games

A collection of simple browser games — currently in development.

Back to Waiting Games · GitHub Repository

Games

  • Asphalt Arkade: A 2D top-down driving game where you escape pursuers to earn points. Choose from 8 vehicles, ranging from a compact car to a rocket, and explore suburbs, downtown, industrial areas, highways, mountains, villages, and the seaside. Crossing the map takes about 2–3 minutes. Tune your engine, turbocharger, tyres, brakes, and vehicle weight.
  • Mattenwurf: A casual skill game about throwing newspapers onto doorsteps. Earn points for accuracy and streaks, while avoiding windows and garden objects. An upgrade helps steady your throws.
  • E-Scooter Clicker: An idle AFK game focused on reaching milestones and upgrading an e-scooter operation.
  • Bastelkarre / MultiplayerRoom: An online sandbox vehicle-builder with modular blocks and templates. Sessions support up to 12 players, and the game includes JSON-based saving. Development started on November 9, 2026.
